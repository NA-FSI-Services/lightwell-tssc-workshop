= Workshop Complete

Congratulations — you have walked the full trusted software supply chain on a dedicated OpenShift cluster.

== What you proved

By completing all seven tracks and passing each Validate Job, you demonstrated that you can, on a fresh cluster:

. **Hummingbird** — verify a published base digest from a trust policy you filled, then mirror it with oc-mirror and verify the internal digest.
. **Lightwell** — author settings.xml so Maven resolves Validated and Remediated coordinates from an enterprise proxy, and pin the remediated GAV from an OSV fixed event.
. **Source** — own the application repo on in-cluster Gitea, bind the Hummingbird digest and Lightwell pins, and merge a live Renovate PR.
. **Hermetic build** — enforce the source forbid-list, wire the prefetch task, tighten build-namespace NetworkPolicy, and produce a BuildConfig image with SBOM.
. **Signed image** — sign keylessly with RHTAS and verify; then place the TUF root and complete a key-based cosign verify (disconnected procedure).
. **Production** — admission rejects unsigned images; the signed digest is committed to the prod GitOps repo and Argo CD prod Application is Healthy.
. **Compliance** — TPA holds the Lightwell GAV-bound VEX; the blast-radius report shows the CVE remediated via the Lightwell pin; one ACS control is on.
