== What a Good Conclusion Includes

* **What You've Learned** (mandatory) -- Recap the learning objectives from every module, so the participant sees the full arc of the lab in one place.
* **References** (mandatory) -- Consolidate every reference cited across all modules here. Individual modules must *not* have their own `== References` section -- this is the only page they belong on.
* **Next Steps** (optional) -- Related labs, further documentation, or practice projects for participants who want to go deeper.
